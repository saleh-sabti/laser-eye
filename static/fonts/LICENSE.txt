Space Grotesk (spacegrotesk-latin.woff2, spacegrotesk-latin-ext.woff2) - designed by
Florian Karsten, distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
https://github.com/floriankarsten/space-grotesk

A variable font (weight axis 300-700; there is no true 900/"black" cut, so the heaviest
weight this app can request is 700). Fetched from Google Fonts and self-hosted here so the
app keeps working with no internet connection at the workbench - see CLAUDE.md.
