Settings

⚙️ Camera & bed

Higher resolution means finer detection precision (more real pixels per mm of bed) at the cost of more CPU/bandwidth for the live preview. Not every camera supports every resolution - if you request one it can't do, it'll just fall back to whatever it actually delivers. Changing this clears your saved calibration and bed area (they're pixel coordinates tied to the old resolution) - redo both after saving.

🧪 Detection method

Classical works with zero training data and is what's validated right now. RF-DETR needs a model trained on your own samples first (see the Training Data page) - if it's not trained yet, the app automatically falls back to classical detection either way.

🔌 GRBL connection

Only used for jogging during calibration (see the Calibration page) - actual burn jobs still run through LightBurn. Only one program can hold the serial port open at a time, so disconnect here before using LightBurn, and vice versa.
