⚠️ ALARM - a limit switch was likely hit. All jogging is blocked until you click Unlock (clears it in place) or Home (drives back to the home corner and re-zeros properly - matches LightBurn's house button).

Icons from LightBurn's jog console we're intentionally not replicating here: the padlock (locks keyboard jogging - not applicable, we don't bind arrow keys), the frame/corner-brackets icon (traces a loaded job's outline - LightBurn owns "the loaded job", we don't), and the lightbulb (fires the laser at low power as a visual aiming pointer). That last one is a deliberate safety choice: this app never fires the laser, under any circumstances - that capability stays exclusively in LightBurn, which already has your machine's safe pointer power configured correctly.
